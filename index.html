Connect your free AI key

This planner writes lessons with Google's free Gemini AI. You only do this once on this device. Your key stays in this browser.

  1. Click the button below. Google's key page opens in a new tab. Sign in with your Gmail.
  2. Click Create API key, then Copy. It starts with AIza.
  3. Come back here and tap Paste my key. If your browser asks, tap Allow. It connects automatically.
